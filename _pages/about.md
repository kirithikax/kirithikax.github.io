I am an MRes student in Artificial Intelligence Enabled Healthcare at University College London (UCL), building upon a First Class Honours BSc in Biomedical Science from Kingston University London. 

My research interests lie at the intersection of clinical decision-support systems and neuro-oncology machine learning. I am actively developing clinical triage architectures—such as the CARE111 protocol—and exploring robust PyTorch pipelines for medical imaging analysis. 

### Research Focus & Technical Expertise
* **Clinical AI & Triage:** Translating complex clinical workflows and NEWS2 scoring systems into automated, high-reliability computational architectures.
* **Machine Learning:** Python, PyTorch, and R for healthcare data analytics and predictive modeling.
* **Clinical Experience:** Grounded perspective gained from clinical triage assessments within high-pressure NHS 111 environments.
